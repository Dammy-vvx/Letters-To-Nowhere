# 💌 Letters to Nowhere

*A little place for all the words you never sent.*

Some things are easier to write than to say. This is a space for those thoughts, unsent letters, unfinished conversations, and everything you wish you could have told someone.

Write it all down. Let it exist somewhere.

No sending. No expectations. Just words.

---

### ✨ A little about this space
- Write letters or emails to anyone — or no one in particular.
- Take your time. There's no right or wrong way to write.
- Your letters stay saved in your browser.
